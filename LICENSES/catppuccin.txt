Catppuccin Mocha / Frappé / Macchiato / Latte palettes — used for the
fish_color_* settings applied at theme load, and for the four bundled
.theme files under themes/ (Damin Mocha / Frappe / Macchiato / Latte).
Hex values verified against catppuccin/palette palette.json.
